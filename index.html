Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

For details specific to your request, please contact the VitalReach team.

How do I request a home visit?

Complete the request form above or call/WhatsApp the team. Your appointment is only confirmed after VitalReach responds and agrees on the arrangements.

Which areas do you cover?

Contact the team with your town or area to confirm current service coverage and any applicable travel arrangements.

Can I request a particular blood test?

Enter the test name in the enquiry form. The team will confirm whether collection can be arranged and whether any preparation instructions apply. Follow instructions from your healthcare professional or laboratory.

When will I receive my results?

Result availability depends on the test and the laboratory processing it. Ask the team for the expected timeline and the approved method for receiving results.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. It sends an enquiry through WhatsApp. Wait for direct confirmation from VitalReach before relying on an appointment.
